Excited to share my internship project: **Salon Experiment & Causal Analysis** 📊

For this project, I explored how data analytics and causal inference can help a fictional 22-outlet salon and spa chain understand whether a business initiative truly improves performance.

What I worked on:
• Created a synthetic dataset with 300,000 appointment records.
• Designed an A/B test with a clear hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, sample-size calculation and test duration.
• Checked randomization balance and sample-ratio mismatch.
• Estimated treatment effects with confidence intervals.
• Applied Difference-in-Differences to evaluate a historical, non-randomized rollout.
• Explored customer segments and used regression adjustment to improve precision.

Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Statsmodels, Matplotlib and Jupyter Notebook.

One important learning: a rise in business performance does not automatically mean an initiative caused it. Seasonality, customer mix and other external factors must also be considered.

**Data transparency:** The project uses a clearly labelled synthetic dataset because original business data was not supplied. The results are for learning and demonstration, not actual salon business claims.

This project strengthened my understanding of data preparation, experimentation, statistical analysis and evidence-based decision-making.
